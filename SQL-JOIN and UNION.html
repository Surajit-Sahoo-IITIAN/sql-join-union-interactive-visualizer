How to understand JOIN

  2. IAMARKS.USN is a Foreign Key referencing STUDENT.USN.
  3. Compare USN in both tables.
  4. Same USN = matching row.
  5. The two circles represent the two tables.
  6. The overlap represents the matching rows.
